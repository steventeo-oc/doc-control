# SOP-QA-0010 Calibration Control

1. Purpose

This procedure defines how measuring and test equipment is calibrated so that measurement results remain traceable and reliable.

2. Scope

It applies to all gauges, torque drivers, multimeters and temperature probes used in production and inspection.

3. Responsibilities

The QA Manager owns the calibration schedule. Department supervisors ensure that equipment in their area carries a valid calibration label.

5. Procedure

5.1 Identification

Every item of measuring equipment receives a unique asset number and a calibration label showing the last calibration date and the next due date.

5.2 Calibration Frequency

Gauges and multimeters are calibrated every 12 months. Torque drivers are calibrated every 6 months. Temperature probes are calibrated every 12 months. Equipment that is dropped or damaged must be recalibrated before further use.

5.3 Out of Tolerance

If equipment is found out of tolerance, the QA Manager raises a nonconformance report on form FORM-QA-0031 and assesses all products measured since the last valid calibration.

6. Records

Calibration certificates are retained for 5 years.
