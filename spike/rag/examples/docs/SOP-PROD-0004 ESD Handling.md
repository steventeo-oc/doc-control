# SOP-PROD-0004 ESD Handling

1. Purpose

This procedure prevents electrostatic discharge damage to sensitive boards and components during assembly.

2. Scope

It applies to every operator and visitor who enters the ESD protected area of the assembly floor.

4. Requirements

4.1 Personal Grounding

Operators wear a wrist strap connected to the workstation ground point. The line lead tests wrist strap continuity at the start of every shift and records the result on form FORM-PROD-0012.

4.2 Handling Rules

Exposed boards are handled only at grounded workstations and are transported in shielded bags. Plastic cups, paper and food are not permitted on the workstation.

4.3 Failed Strap Test

An operator whose wrist strap fails the continuity test must stop work and replace the strap before handling any board.
